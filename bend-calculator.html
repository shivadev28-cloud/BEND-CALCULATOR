Key design rules to remember
Rule 01
Bend Allowance ≠ Bend Deduction. These are often confused on shop floors. BA = arc length of neutral axis. BD = amount to subtract from virtual sharp dimensions.
Rule 02
K-Factor range 0–1. K = 0 = neutral axis at inside surface. K = 0.5 = true mid-thickness. In practice, most metals fall between 0.3 and 0.5.
Rule 03
Bend Angle α vs Opening Angle β. α is the outside angle (what CAD uses). β = 180° − α. Mixing these up causes flat pattern errors.
Rule 04
For highest accuracy — use measured BD tables. K-Factors are approximations. Manufacturer-generated Bend Deduction Tables from real test bends are the shop-floor gold standard.
Rule 05
Virtual Sharp is your measurement reference. The exact bend line can't be measured physically. Always measure flanges to their virtual sharp intersection point.
Rule 06
DIN 6935 KDIN ≠ Standard K-Factor. KDIN is used only inside the DIN Bend Compensation formula. It is not interchangeable with K-Factor in the standard Bend Allowance equation.
